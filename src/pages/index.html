About the company
Technology

Continuous coil
anodizing.

Anodizing turns the surface of aluminium into a hard, protective oxide layer that is part of the metal, not a coating on top. On our lines the whole coil runs through every bath without stopping.

Products

Coils and sheets.
Three finishes.

Mill, Brushed and Bright, in natural silver, metallic shades from gold to bronze, or colours like red, blue and green. The colour is sealed inside the oxide layer, so it does not peel or fade.

Supplied in coils, sheets or strips, 0.2 to 2 mm thick and up to 1500 mm wide.
