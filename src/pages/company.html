Founded in Italy in 1946 and run by the same family for three generations. We anodize aluminium coils and sheets for manufacturers worldwide.

Three generations.
One know-how.

Since 1946 the company has passed from one generation of the family to the next, and so has the know-how: how the metal behaves in each bath, and how to keep a colour steady along thousands of metres of coil.

The family still runs the company day to day, close to the plant and to the customers.

We anodize aluminium
in continuous coil.

Everything happens on one 16,000 m² site, with two automated anodizing lines and our own cutting machine.

The coil runs through every bath without stopping, so surface and colour stay the same from the first metre to the last. We deliver in coils, or cut in-house into sheets and strips. How continuous coil anodizing works
