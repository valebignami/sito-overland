A family company founded in 1946 near Milan. We anodize aluminium coils and sheets for manufacturers around the world.

What we do

We specialise in continuous coil anodizing. The aluminium coil runs through every stage of the process on one of our two automated lines, from raw metal to finished surface, so the surface and the colour stay the same along the whole coil.

We supply the aluminium in coils, or cut into sheets and strips, in a range of dimensions, anodic layer thicknesses and colours. How continuous coil anodizing works
