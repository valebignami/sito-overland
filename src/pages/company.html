A family company founded in Italy in 1946. We anodize aluminium coils and sheets for manufacturers around the world.

We anodize aluminium
in continuous coil.

The aluminium coil runs through every stage of the process on one of our two automated lines, from raw metal to finished surface, so the surface and the colour stay the same along the whole coil.

We supply the aluminium in coils, or cut into sheets and strips, in a range of dimensions, anodic layer thicknesses and colours. How continuous coil anodizing works
