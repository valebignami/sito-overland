A family company founded near Milan in 1946. We anodize aluminium coils and sheets for manufacturers around the world, and we work to do it with as light a footprint as we can.

What we do

We specialize in continuous coil anodizing. We buy rolled aluminium and turn it into anodized coils and sheets on two automated lines. The coil runs through every stage without stopping, from raw metal to finished surface, so the surface and the colour stay the same from the first metre to the last.

We deliver it in coils, or cut into sheets and strips, in the dimensions, anodic layer and colour each project needs. How continuous coil anodizing works

Making more
with less.

Anodized aluminium is a material that lasts and can be recycled again and again. We want the way we make it to be just as responsible.

Saving resources is not an add-on for us. It is part of how our lines are built and run: at every stage we look at energy, water, chemicals and material, and keep finding ways to use less.

Solar energy

Photovoltaic panels on our roof generate clean electricity that helps power the plant.

Recycling built in

Aluminium offcuts and trimmings are collected and sent back to be remelted into new metal, and packaging is separated for recycling.

Water and chemicals

We recover and reuse process water and chemicals wherever the process allows, and treat all wastewater before it leaves the site.

An efficient process

A continuous line needs fewer chemicals, less water and less energy than batch anodizing for the same surface.

No paints or solvents

Anodizing grows the surface out of the aluminium itself. There is no paint, no coating and no solvent to release emissions.

Made to last, then recycled

A surface that does not peel, fade or corrode needs replacing less often, and at the end of its life the aluminium can be recycled without losing its properties.
