The material
behind the finish.

Anodizing grows a protective oxide layer that is part of the aluminium itself. The metal keeps its lightness and gains a surface that lasts.

Light but strong

About a third of the weight of stainless steel.

Corrosion and UV resistant

It does not rust, chip, peel or patina, indoors or outdoors.

Hard-wearing

The oxide layer resists scratches and wear better than bare aluminium.

Colour that stays

Colours sit inside the oxide rather than on top, so they do not fade or flake.

Non-combustible

It does not burn, which suits interiors and public spaces.

Resilient

It flexes under impact loads instead of cracking.

Reflective

Among the most reflective metal surfaces available.

100% recyclable

It can be recycled at the end of its life without losing its properties.
